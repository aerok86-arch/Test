## Notes on AI Tells

Common phrases like "Thanks for reaching out!" aren't inherently AI-generated if they're your genuine voice. The real tell is *clustering* — multiple warm phrases stacked together, which humans rarely do. Note any phrases here that appear in your samples so the skill knows they're authentically yours:

- [e.g., "I always say 'Happy to help' — that's genuinely me"]
