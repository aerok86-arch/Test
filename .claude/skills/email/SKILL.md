## Two Email Registers

### Register A — Quick / Transactional
Ongoing exchanges where both parties know the context.
- Direct opener, no warm-up
- 2–4 short paragraphs, 1–2 sentences each
- Minimal formatting
- Confident, informal tone

### Register B — Developed / Business
First contact or business development.
- Warmer opener, fuller context-setting
- Appropriate list formatting when needed
- Open-door closing
- More polished but still personal

---

## Drafting Workflow

1. Read the samples in `references/samples.md` before drafting — every time
2. Identify the relationship and appropriate register (A or B)
3. Draft once, then cut ruthlessly
4. Check for AI tells (see below) and remove them
5. Present to user

---

## AI Tells to Strip

**Phrases:** "Hope this email finds you well," "Just wanted to circle back," "I'd be more than happy to," "Please don't hesitate to reach out," "As per my previous email"

**Words:** leverage, navigate, seamless, robust, foster, holistic, streamline, utilize

**Structural tells:** formulaic em-dash usage, rule-of-three lists, excessive gratitude, restating what they said back to them, multiple warm phrases stacked at the start
