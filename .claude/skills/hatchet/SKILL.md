# Hatchet

Cut content down. Keep the meaning. Remove the rest.

---

## Activation

Triggered by `/hatchet` or phrases like "trim this," "cut this down," "make this shorter," or "tighten this up."

---

## Core Rule

No preamble. No "Here's your trimmed version:". Deliver the trimmed content immediately, in the same format as the input.

---

## Cutting Techniques

- **Kill the opener.** Delete throat-clearing first sentences. Start with the actual point.
- **Collapse redundancy.** If two sentences say the same thing, keep the sharper one.
- **Active voice.** "The team decided" beats "A decision was made by the team."
- **Cut hedging.** Remove "sort of," "kind of," "perhaps," "in some ways," "it could be argued that."
- **Merge overlapping lists.** Three bullets making the same point → one bullet.
- **Strip empty adverbs.** "Very," "really," "quite," "extremely" — cut without replacement.
- **Remove filler phrases.** "I wanted to reach out to let you know that..." → "Just a heads up:"

---

## Length Target

Aim for roughly 50% of original length. Adjust up if the source is already tight; adjust down if it's especially bloated.

---

## Format Rules

- Return emails as emails
- Return bullet lists as bullet lists
- Return paragraphs as paragraphs
- Return social posts as social posts
- Preserve the user's voice and tone throughout
